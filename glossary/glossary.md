# Glossary

## Art Holds What Time Takes
The category and central idea. Heirloom art as emotional care. The practice of transforming love, beauty, and fleeting moments into timeless works that preserve life’s magic and bring comfort to the human soul.

## The Visible Love Practice
The core framework. Surrender. See. Honor. Reveal. Hold. A philosophy for recognizing love and giving it permanent artistic form.

## Visible Love Builds Belonging
The core principle. The conviction that making love visible in the home affirms family identity and secures emotional connection across generations.

## Heirloom Art as Emotional Care
The category differentiator. European elegance, high-fashion visual mastery, deep human empathy, faith-led purpose, and generational permanence working as one.

## The Collector
Discerning families and couples who value family identity, emotional connection, and classical beauty. Defined by values, not wealth alone. They prefer fewer, deeply meaningful pieces over hundreds of digital files.
