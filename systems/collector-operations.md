# System: Collector & Commission Operations

**Fine Art Collector:** Seeks artwork with meaning, presence, authorship, and permanence. Not filling a wall. Collecting permanence.
**Private Commission Collector:** Recognizes that time moves. Seeks to transform a moment into physical permanence. Philosophy: *Art holds what time takes.*

**Commission Journey:** Inquiry → Conversation → Artistic Direction → Commission Acceptance → Creation → Artist Refinement → Viewing → Selection → Production → Provenance → Installation/Delivery.
**Pricing/Objection Standards:** Value justified through artist, authorship, scale, edition, rarity, presentation, provenance, permanence. Never through production cost.
