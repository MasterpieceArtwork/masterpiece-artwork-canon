# System: Provenance & Archival Standards

- **Edition Standards:** Archival aluminum/metal, museum-grade production, numbered, signed, finite runs, permanent closure upon sell-out.
- **Certificate of Authenticity Rules:** Standardized format, includes title, artist, medium, dimensions, year, edition number, total size, signature, Masterpiece Artwork seal.
- **Archive Procedures:** Preserve original artwork records, edition records, COAs, provenance, early drawings/paintings/photography/mixed-media development, sketches, artist writings, licensing history, published products, press coverage, exhibitions, collector placements, development of The Masterpiece Method™.
