# System: Provenance & Archival Standards

- **Edition Standards:** Archival-quality production standards, numbered, signed, finite edition policy, permanent closure upon sell-out.
- **Certificate of Authenticity Rules:** Standardized format, includes title, artist, medium, dimensions, year, edition number, total size, signature, Masterpiece Artwork seal.
- **Archive Procedures:** Preserve original artwork records, edition records, COAs, provenance, early developmental work, artist writings, licensing history, press coverage, and collector placements.
