# Governance Standards

## Canonical Foundation

Masterpiece Artwork is governed by the following foundation:

**Category:** Fine Art  
**Brand:** Masterpiece Artwork  
**Practice:** Multidisciplinary  
**Differentiator:** The Masterpiece Method™

**ART, MADE INEVITABLE.**

The medium serves the work.

## Canonical Authority

This repository is the canonical record of Masterpiece Artwork's brand architecture, artistic philosophy, governing frameworks, terminology, and long-term standards.

The current Canon supersedes conflicting legacy documentation.

Historical materials may be preserved as historical records, but they do not override the current Canon.

## Core Language

Masterpiece Artwork may be described as:

- Masterpiece Artwork
- Fine Art
- Multidisciplinary practice
- The Masterpiece Method™
- Artists
- Artwork
- Collections
- Collectors
- Private Commissions
- Provenance
- Editions
- Archive
- Trade
- Galleries
- Exhibitions
- Art Fairs
- Publishing

Avoid positioning Masterpiece Artwork as a photography studio, portrait studio, Fine Art House, Art House, or institution.

Do not describe individual works as “masterpieces” as a self-declared quality claim.

Do not make blanket “No AI” claims. Medium and process descriptions must reflect the individual artwork truthfully.

## Artist Independence

Masterpiece Artwork protects and develops the distinct artistic voice of each artist.

The Masterpiece Method™ establishes a shared artistic standard; it does not require stylistic sameness.

## Medium Independence

No single medium or technology defines Masterpiece Artwork.

Photography, drawing, painting, digital painting, generative processes, compositing, mixed media, and future tools may be used according to the needs of the work.

**The Masterpiece Method™ governs the work—not the medium.**

## Canon Changes

Changes to foundational Canon should be deliberate and infrequent.

Before changing the category, brand architecture, The Masterpiece Method™, or another foundational principle, the proposed change should be evaluated against:

1. Artistic truth
2. Long-term brand coherence
3. Existing canonical architecture
4. Collector clarity
5. The 20-Year Rule

Temporary marketing strategies, pricing, edition quantities, revenue targets, sales channels, campaigns, technologies, and operating tactics should not automatically become permanent Canon.

## Legacy Material

Superseded frameworks and category definitions must not be reintroduced as current Canon.

This includes legacy positioning that defines Masterpiece Artwork primarily through photography, portraiture, family artwork, or Private Commissions.

**ART HOLDS WHAT TIME TAKES.** remains the philosophy of the Private Commission practice. It is not the category definition of Masterpiece Artwork.

## Governing Principle

**Masterpiece is the name. The Method is our standard. The work speaks for itself.**
