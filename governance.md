# Governance & Voice Standards

## Category Claim
Multidisciplinary fine art governed by The Masterpiece Method™.

## Voice Rules
- **We Use:** Masterpiece Artwork, The Masterpiece Method™, Collect the work, Private commissions, The Gallery, The collection, Provenance, Edition.
- **We Never Use:** Photography studio, Portrait studio, Art house, Fine Art House, "Our masterpieces," "Own a masterpiece," "Commission your masterpiece," "No AI" (as a blanket claim), Institution.
- **Tone:** Restraint. Explains work, Method, provenance, and artistic decisions without telling collectors how to feel. Authoritative but unassuming. Timeless.

## Update Protocol
- New frameworks or credibility anchors must be mined by Fuller before installation.
- Category claim changes require client confirmation and version increment.
- MANIFESTO.md is produced by Stephen and will be added to this repository upon completion.

## Boundary Enforcement
This repository documents a multidisciplinary fine art practice. It does not represent a photography studio, an art house, or an institution. The medium serves the work.
