# Framework: Medium-Labeling & Process Disclosure Standards

**Definition:** Truthful, work-specific labeling. Transparent per artwork. No brand-wide blanket claims. Process described only when it accurately reflects the individual work.

**Components:** Accurate medium combination labeling (e.g., Photography · Digital Painting · Mixed Media), per-work process disclosure, rejection of blanket "No AI" claims.

**Function:** Maintains transparency, technological neutrality, and collector trust. Applied to every released artwork's documentation and provenance file.
