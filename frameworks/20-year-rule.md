# Framework: The 20-Year Rule

**Definition:** Masterpiece Artwork must remain broad enough to still make sense twenty years from now, regardless of shifts in medium, technology, location, or business model.

**Components:** Defined by artistic intention, authorship, The Masterpiece Method™, quality, presentation, provenance, and collector sustainability.

**Function:** Ensures brand architecture outlives technological and market shifts. Applied to strategic planning, collection development, publishing, and partnership architecture.
