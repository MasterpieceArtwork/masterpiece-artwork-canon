# Framework: Artist Identity

**Definition:** Masterpiece Artwork protects and develops the distinct artistic voice of each artist. The Masterpiece Method™ establishes a shared artistic standard; it does not require stylistic sameness.

**Components:** Individual authorship, recognizable visual signatures, distinct collections, unified brand standard.

**Function:** Ensures collectors can recognize an Ela Hersberger or Larry Hersberger work independently, while operating under one house.
