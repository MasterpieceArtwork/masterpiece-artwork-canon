# Glossary

## Art Holds What Time Takes
The category and central idea. Heirloom art as emotional care. The practice of transforming fleeting moments into timeless works that preserve life's magic and bring comfort to the human soul.

## My Process
The core framework. Surrender. See. Honor. Reveal. Hold. A sequential practice for recognizing love and giving it permanent artistic form.

## Heirloom Art as Emotional Care
The category differentiator. European elegance, high-fashion visual mastery, deep human empathy, faith-led purpose, and generational permanence working as one.

## The Collector
Discerning families and couples who value family identity, emotional connection, and classical beauty. Defined by values, not wealth alone. They prefer fewer, deeply meaningful pieces over hundreds of digital files.

## Permanent Belonging
The conviction that making love visible in the home secures belonging across generations. Families do not need more photographs. They need a permanent record of lineage.
