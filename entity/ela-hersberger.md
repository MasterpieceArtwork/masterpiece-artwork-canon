# Ela Hersberger

## Entity Declaration
Ela Hersberger is an Artist of Hope and Healing. She operates at the intersection of classical European elegance, high-fashion visual mastery, and faith-led emotional care. Her body of work spans commissioned family artwork, published writing, and advocacy through the Don't Fear Me Foundation. Her lived experience — from attending galas in a Polish castle theater to moving to the US with no language, and choosing life for Anna Rose against medical fear — is the foundation of her practice.

## Declaration
Ela Hersberger creates family artwork that preserves love for the future and makes belonging visible in the present.
