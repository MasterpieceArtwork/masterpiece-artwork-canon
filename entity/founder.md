# Founder Declaration

**Ela & Larry Hersberger**
*Multidisciplinary Fine Artists*

Founded by artists Ela and Larry Hersberger, Masterpiece Artwork presents original and limited-edition fine art alongside a selective Private Commission practice.

**Ela Hersberger:** Classical European painting foundation, Old Masters influence, multidisciplinary progression from drawing to painting to photography to mixed media.
**Larry Hersberger:** Art of Magic and Light® expertise, international installation experience across 50+ countries, historical licensing and retail distribution.

One medium did not replace another. Each became another instrument available to the artist. Different artists. Different collections. One brand. One Method.
